    Walkthrough

    1. Use Steady Pace for most of the trip. Every move or rest increases your thirst. Drink when the game warns you, or sooner if you have water to spare.
    2. Full Speed widens the gap quickly but adds 25 percentage points of fatigue. At 100%, your camel collapses. Rest while you have a lead. A rest removes 55 points of fatigue but the raiders keep moving.
    3. Oases refill your six drinks. At the two forks, the dunes cover more ground and tire the camel. The well trail is slower, but the well may hold water.
    4. Reach 322 km before the raiders close the gap or your supplies fail. Sandstorms and water finds vary between runs.
